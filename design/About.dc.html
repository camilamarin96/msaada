Msaada means help.

Msaada is a circular community support initiative. We collect useful items people no longer need, repair and reuse them, and redistribute or sell them at affordable prices to refugees and host community members.

Msaada · مساعدة = help

Why Msaada was created

Useful things are thrown away every day, while families nearby go without blankets, beds, school books or a working phone. At the same time, many young people are looking for practical skills and a way into work.

Msaada connects these needs. Donated items are collected, repaired by trained young people, then sold cheaply or given to those in need. Money from sales pays for the next round.

Our mission

To collect, reuse and redistribute useful items, reduce waste, and support individuals and communities in need, while creating skills and opportunities for young people.

Our vision

A caring, sustainable and inclusive community where resources are shared, people are supported, and everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

Sustainability and social support work better together.

A mattress that stays in use is one less mattress in the dump. At Msaada, that same mattress goes to a family who needs it, after a young trainee has checked or fixed it.

What we stand for

  • Solidarity

    What one household no longer needs, another can use.

  • Sustainability

    Keeping useful items in use and out of the waste stream for as long as possible.

  • Inclusion

    Refugee and host communities alike, with dignity and fair access for everyone.

  • Opportunity

    Practical skills and real work for young people through repair and preparation.

  • Community

    Built with and for the people we serve.

Where your donation ends up

A donated blanket keeps a family warm. A repaired phone gives a trainee practice and a buyer a phone that works. Small things like these add up across the settlement.

  • Vulnerable families

    Household essentials (beds, blankets, utensils) at prices they can manage.

  • Children and young people

    Clothes, shoes and toys for children; repair skills and opportunities for youth.

  • Students and schools

    Textbooks, exercise books and devices that help learners keep learning.

  • Refugee and host communities

    Fair, affordable access to goods for everyone living in and around the settlement.

  • People in need

    Items that can’t be resold are given directly to those who need them most.

  • Community initiatives & local organisations

    Partners who share resources and reach more people together.
