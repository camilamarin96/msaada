Step by step

  1. 1

    You contact us

    Donors get in touch to tell us which items they would like to donate.

  2. 2

    We collect

    The team arranges a convenient collection time and collects items from the donor’s location.

  3. 3

    We receive and sort

    Donated items are received, checked, cleaned and sorted.

    Useful items kept in use

  4. 4

    Transport to the Msaada store

    Sorted items are transported to the Msaada store in the settlement.

  5. 5

    Youth repair and prepare

    Young people receive practical training and repair items so they can be reused.

    Repaired, not discardedSkills for young people

  6. 6

    Msaada store

    Repaired and quality-checked items are displayed at the store.

  7. 7

    Affordable sale or redistribution

    Items are made available at affordable prices to refugees and other community members. Other items may be distributed to those in need.

    Access to essential goods

  8. 8

    Income supports sustainability

    Money from sales pays for transport and the next collections.

    Funds the next collection

    Then back to step 2, and the circle keeps turning.

Environmental value

  • Useful items stay in use for longer
  • Repair instead of replacement
  • Less waste in the community

Social value

  • Affordable essentials for refugees and host communities
  • Practical skills for young people
  • Free items for those most in need
