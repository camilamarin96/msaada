What we run

  1. Youth repair & practical skills training

    Hands-on training where young people learn to check, fix and prepare donated items, from phones and electronics to furniture, so they can be used again.

  2. Community learning activities

    Open sessions where community members share knowledge and learn practical, everyday skills together.

  3. Repair & reuse workshops

    Practical workshops on repairing, upcycling and finding new uses for everyday items.

  4. Sustainability awareness sessions

    Conversations about throwing less away and keeping things in use for longer.

  5. Other community engagement

    Collection drives, open days or activities with partner organisations.
